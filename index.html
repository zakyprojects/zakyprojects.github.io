Live · Beta Founder & developer

AscendDay

A gamified self-improvement app. Step up every day.

AscendDay turns daily habits, focus sessions, journaling, skills and workouts into points. Friends compete on weekly and monthly leagues, and a new season starts every 90 days.

The problem
Habit apps are easy to quit and easy to cheat, so points stop meaning anything.
What I built
Points are calculated on the server, not in the browser. Data syncs across devices and works offline. The app installs on phones and sends push reminders.
Under the hood
Postgres with row-level security, scheduled jobs, edge functions, automated tests, and a CI check before every deploy.
